Thandol Span, our own masons' work, carries the Wetlands road into a country of green slopes and old fortifications. Thoradin's Wall marks the western passage toward Hillsbrad. Stromgarde's great walls rise above the south-western coast.

Refuge Pointe lies among the central hills for the Alliance, while Hammerfall holds the north-east for the Horde. Stromgarde itself is divided between surviving defenders, Syndicate forces and ogres. A wall that looks whole on the map may guard nothing safe.

The binding circles are conspicuous landmarks on the open ground. Faldir's Cove is tucked below the southern cliffs, much less apparent from the road than from the sea.
