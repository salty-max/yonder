Ironforge's gate opens from the snow into a city arranged around the Great Forge. I will not pretend to be impartial about it. The main ring joins the Commons, the High Seat, Tinker Town and the Hall of Explorers. Knowing which direction to take around it saves a good deal of walking.

The Deeprun Tram leaves from Tinker Town for Stormwind, and gryphons depart beside the Great Forge. The League's hall is a quieter destination.

[forever] Below the city, the caverns beneath Old Ironforge lead to the Hall of Thanes. Dark Iron intruders have found a way into its lower vaults. The city now has a second front directly below the king's feet.
