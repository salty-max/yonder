Durotar's red gullies channel the roads between its settlements. It is hard, dry country, and the orcs build in it better than I expected. Razor Hill stands at their meeting, with Orgrimmar to the north, Sen'jin Village toward the south coast and the crossing into the Barrens to the west.

The Valley of Trials lies apart from the main road. Tiragarde Keep occupies the eastern coast, while the Echo Isles are visible offshore from Darkspear country without being safely in Darkspear hands.

Outside Orgrimmar, the zeppelin towers give the approach a second skyline above the canyon walls. Here the crossings to other lands go by air, not by sea.
