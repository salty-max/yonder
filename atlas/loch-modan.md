A long lake held back by the Stonewrought Dam, the finest piece of dwarven engineering outside Ironforge, and I will hear no argument. Thelsamar sits on the western shore on the road from Dun Morogh, and the road goes on north to the Wetlands and south to the Badlands.

Stone kings guard the pass at the Valley of Kings. The rest of the country has no such guards: troggs in Stonesplinter Valley, kobolds in the mines, and ogres in the Mo'grosh Stronghold in the north-east hills.

The high elves of the Farstrider Lodge keep to themselves and know the country well. Ask them before hunting.
