The road out of Hillsbrad climbs from green foothills into snow and the remains of Alterac's kingdom. Strahnbrad is occupied by the Syndicate, while ogres hold much of the ruined capital. A tower on a ridge makes a good landmark, but no shelter.

[classic] Beside Lordamere Lake, Dalaran's violet dome is visible across the lower country. The mages work behind it. From the road, a traveller sees only the dome and its walls.

[forever] Dalaran stands beside Lordamere Lake, where Archmage Modera seeks help with a crisis inside the city. Disrupted ley lines have turned its constructs destructive. From the road, the city looks orderly, with guards at its gates.

Higher up, yeti caves and snowfields interrupt the mountain paths. The lake gives a fixed point below. Among the ridges, the next turning is often hidden. Good mountain country, by a dwarf's measure.
