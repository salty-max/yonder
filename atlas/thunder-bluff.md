The tauren's city stands on the mesas of northern Mulgore. Great lifts carry travellers up from the plain, and rope bridges join the rises at the top.

Elder Rise, Spirit Rise and Hunter Rise are the landmarks to steer by. The bank and the auction house are on the central rise, and the wind riders fly from the tower.

The bridges sway, and I prefer stone underfoot. Do not look down, and do not run.
