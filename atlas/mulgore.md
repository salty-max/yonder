Green plains under a big sky, and hardly a mountain in sight: this is the tauren's home. Thunder Bluff stands on its mesas in the north. Bloodhoof Village sits in the south, and the road runs between them and out east to the Barrens.

The plains are kind, mostly. The gnolls of Palemane Rock, the harpies of Windfury Ridge and the Venture Company's mine are not, and the tauren guard their wells for good reason. The dwarves at the Bael'dun digsite dig where the tauren would rather they did not.

Red Rocks, in the east, is a resting place of the tauren's honoured dead. Walk there quietly.
