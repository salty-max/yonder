Elwynn's main road runs from Stormwind through Goldshire and east to Redridge. Northshire occupies a sheltered valley to the north, while the western crossing leads into Westfall. Farms, bridges and lakes give this country the clearest bearings I know.

The mines are less orderly than the fields above them: kobolds occupy Fargodeep and Jasperlode, and Defias activity reaches well into the forest. In the south-west, the Riverpaw camps make the open woodland around the river dangerous ground.

Stormwind is never far away in miles. To a farmer waiting for help with a mine or a camp, it is a long way.
