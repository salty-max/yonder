Snow, stone and dwarves, in that order. The valleys run east and west below the peaks that hide Ironforge, and the one road follows them. It runs from Coldridge Valley through the tunnel to Kharanos, up to the city's great gate, and on east through the pass to Loch Modan.

Kharanos has the Thunderbrew distillery and an inn with a good fire. West of it the ground belongs to the Frostmane trolls, and further west stands Gnomeregan, its halls poisoned. The gnomes have not gone home.

In a snowstorm, keep to the road and carry a spare cloak. The drifts are deep, and the troggs of Coldridge pay no heed to a lost traveller.
