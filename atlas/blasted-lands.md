The road south from the Swamp of Sorrows reaches Nethergarde Keep above a red waste. Its walls are the soundest stonework for many miles. The fortress watches the Dark Portal, whose surviving frame stands farther south in the country the invasion scarred.

Dreadmaul ogres occupy the western hills, and demons hold the Tainted Scar. Beyond the keep, a traveller has few friendly landmarks to work between. The Portal is easy enough to find. Finding a place near it where anyone could rest is harder.
