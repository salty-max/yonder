Centaur clans occupy the open country of Kalimdor, their villages and warbands spread across the Barrens, Desolace and Thousand Needles. The tauren have fought them for generations. In Desolace, the names Gelkis, Magram and Maraudine mark rival claims as well as different camps.

A traveller can bargain with some of these clans, though friendship with one is liable to cost the friendship of another. Their shamans speak of the earth, and Theradras has her worshippers among them.

Their ancestry leads back to Theradras and Zaetar, the son of Cenarius whose own children killed him. Maraudon holds that family history in a particularly unhappy form.
