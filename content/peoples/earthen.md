The earthen are beings of living stone, made by the titans to shape the deep places of the world. In Uldaman some of them still walk the halls of the titans' vault, guarding it as they were made to.

The platinum discs in that vault say the dwarves descend from them. The earthen attack on sight all the same.

The League records them with respect, and with caution.
