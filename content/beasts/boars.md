Boars turn up in the red gullies of Durotar as readily as in Elwynn's green fields. The colouring changes, and a crag boar has a thick coat against the snow, but the tusks give the same unmistakable outline.

Westfall's goretusks are a nuisance to the farms and a useful supply of meat.
