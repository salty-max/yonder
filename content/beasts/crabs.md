A crab carries a little fortress along the shore. The shell does most of the defending, the claws do the rest, and the whole arrangement moves sideways with admirable assurance.

Sand crawlers and surf crawlers occupy beaches across both continents. Cooks value their meat, while an undisturbed stretch of sand gives a naturalist something worth watching: a creature going about its affairs at the boundary between land and sea.
