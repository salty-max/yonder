The wendigos of Dun Morogh inhabit snowy caves such as the Grizzled Den, where white fur disappears against the snow. The size of their hands is less easily overlooked.

Local hunters want their hides and manes. To a young dwarf, the hills beyond a settlement can seem an extension of home. A wendigo at a cave mouth gives those hills a rather different scale.
